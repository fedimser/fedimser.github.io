Tetris (2011)

A classic "Tetris" game.
You have to direct falling tetrominoes in order to form solid layers. If that happens, the layer will disappear.
More layers - a higher score. A higher score - a higher level. A higher level - faster falling tetrominoes.
If tetrominoes have no space to fall, the game is over.
There are 5 types of tetromino in the game.
You may change the colors and size of the field.
Controls: mouse/ keyboard arrows.
Language: Russian.

Download
.NET Framework 4 is required.
